## Critical

### C1. Contribution 1 and the conclusion misstate the native-API failure mix

- **Where:** `paper/main.tex:88` and `paper/main.tex:233`.
- **Found by:** consistency (inline), technical (inline), outsider. §4 was fixed for this on 2026-10-18; the front and the back of the paper were not.
- **What is wrong:** Both sentences attach "mostly under-calls and wrong final states" to the 27.0% native-API result (54/200). `tab:failfull` for NFC is empty turn 59, under-call 19, state mismatch 68. Under-call is the smallest bucket. §4 (`main.tex:140`) already says this: the native API has more empty turns (59) and far fewer under-calls (19). Under-calls become large for constrained decoding (70), the tool-name automaton (54), and the best fine-tune (59).
- **Suggested fix:** In both sentences, say the native-API failures are mostly empty turns and wrong final states, and that forcing valid calls or fine-tuning shifts the failures into under-calls. Use the counts already in `tab:failfull`. Do not add a new count.

### C2. The abstract and the conclusion overstate the null test

- **Where:** abstract `paper/main.tex:58` ("points to the training data, rather than the training pipeline, as the main suspect"); conclusion `paper/main.tex:233` ("a null test points to the training data rather than the pipeline"). The careful sentence is `paper/main.tex:196`.
- **Found by:** technical (inline), writing (inline), outsider.
- **What is wrong:** §6 says the null test rules out the template, merge, quantize, and serve steps at a small update budget (39 updates on 156 logged answers, 15/20). The rebuilt mix differs in content and in length (22,000 steps, 2/20), "so its data are the leading suspect but not the only one." The abstract and the conclusion drop that second half. A reader who stops at the abstract gets a cause the results section does not claim.
- **Suggested fix:** Use the §6 hedge in both places: the control fine-tune makes the data the leading suspect, and the training length also differed. Keep 15.0% and 26.5% if they stay; do not add a new update count in the abstract. "39 updates" and "22,000 steps" are already in the body and the scoreboard row name; do not strengthen them.
